# Aurora Labs Remote Work Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All regular employees whose role is eligible for remote work

This policy describes how remote work operates at Aurora Labs. It covers the number
of remote days allowed, the equipment the company reimburses, the coworking
allowance, and what the company expects in terms of availability and travel. It sits
alongside the employee handbook, which sets the general expense limits and approval
chain that any reimbursement under this policy must also follow.

---

## 1. Eligibility

A role is eligible for remote work when the employee can perform their duties
without being physically present at an office every day. Eligibility is confirmed by
the employee's manager and recorded when the role is posted or when an existing
employee requests a change.

Some roles are not eligible. These include front of house, hardware test bench
roles that require physical access to equipment, and any role explicitly marked
office based in the job description. An employee in a non eligible role who wishes to
change to remote work must apply through the internal transfer process.

---

## 2. Remote days and office attendance

### 2.1 Standard arrangement

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week. The remaining two
days are spent at the employee's assigned office. The company operates anchor days,
which are Tuesday and Thursday, and every remote employee is expected in the office
on both anchor days unless they have arranged otherwise with their manager.

### 2.2 Compressed schedules

An employee may request a compressed schedule that concentrates the two office days
into a single week every fortnight. This requires the manager's written agreement and
must be reviewed after three months to confirm it is working for the team.

### 2.3 Working from another country

An employee may work from another country for up to thirty consecutive days per
calendar year, provided the employee remains tax resident in their country of
employment and the arrangement does not create a permanent establishment risk for
Aurora Labs. Requests are submitted to People Operations at least thirty days in
advance and are approved or declined in writing.

Working from another country does not change the employee's salary, and the employee
remains subject to the laws of their country of employment.

---

## 3. Home office equipment

### 3.1 Reimbursable list

Aurora Labs reimburses the following home office equipment for eligible employees:

- Desk, including standing and sit-stand desks
- Ergonomic office chair
- Monitor, up to a maximum of two
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset
- Desk lamp with adjustable light
- Laptop stand
- Webcam, if the employee's laptop does not include one
- Surge protector for the desk area

Computers, laptops and mobile phones are provided by the company through the IT
department and are never reimbursed under this policy. Internet service is not
reimbursable under this list; employees who need a higher bandwidth connection should
raise it with their manager, and the company may fund an upgrade where the role
requires it.

### 3.2 Approval path and limits

The home office equipment list above is reimbursed under the expense limits and
approval chain set out in the employee handbook. The handbook defines the spending
thresholds and who must approve an expense above each threshold, and those same
thresholds apply to every item on the list in section 3.1.

The limits specific to home office equipment are:

- Up to 1,200 USD may be claimed in an employee's first year of remote working, to
  set up the initial workspace.
- Up to 400 USD may be claimed every three years thereafter to replace worn items.
- A single item costing more than 500 USD requires the employee's manager to approve
  the purchase in writing before it is bought. This is in addition to any approval
  the handbook requires for that spending threshold.

Claims are submitted through the expense system with the receipt attached, under the
home office category, and must be filed within sixty days of purchase. Items bought
without the required pre-approval may be declined.

### 3.3 Ownership and return

Equipment reimbursed under this policy belongs to the employee. If an employee leaves
Aurora Labs, no equipment needs to be returned. Company provided equipment from
section 3.1, such as laptops, must be returned through the IT offboarding process.

---

## 4. Coworking allowance

### 4.1 Who may use it

An employee whose assigned office is more than fifty kilometres from their home may
claim a coworking allowance instead of travelling to the office on anchor days. The
allowance covers a desk at a coworking space of the employee's choosing.

### 4.2 Amount and process

The allowance is up to 150 USD per month, claimed monthly through the expense system
under the coworking category, with the coworking provider's invoice attached. The
allowance is a reimbursement of actual cost up to the monthly cap, and unused
portions do not roll over to the next month.

### 4.3 Conditions

The employee must remain available during the core hours described in section 5 and
must still attend the office for team events and all hands meetings. The coworking
allowance is not available to employees who live within fifty kilometres of their
assigned office, and an employee who moves closer to an office must stop claiming the
allowance from the month after the move.

---

## 5. Availability and communication

### 5.1 Core hours

Every employee is expected to be reachable and working during core hours, which are
10:00 to 16:00 in the time zone of their assigned office. Outside core hours, an
employee may set their own schedule provided their commitments are met and their
calendar reflects when they are working.

### 5.2 Response expectations

Messages sent during core hours should be acknowledged within four working hours.
Urgent matters are raised through the on call channel described in the IT security
policy, not through direct message. An employee who will be unavailable for more than
two hours during core hours must set their status and leave a note on the team
channel.

### 5.3 Meetings

Meetings with more than two attendees must have an agenda sent in advance and notes
shared afterwards. Any meeting where at least one participant is remote must be run
with a shared document open, so that remote participants contribute equally.

---

## 6. Travel and in-person gatherings

### 6.1 All hands meetings

Aurora Labs holds an all hands meeting twice per year, in April and October. Travel
and accommodation for the all hands are paid by the company for every employee,
including remote employees. Attendance is expected unless an employee has a
conflicting commitment agreed in advance with their manager.

### 6.2 Team gatherings

Teams may gather in person once per quarter for planning or social purposes. The
cost of a team gathering is covered by the team's own budget and follows the same
expense approval chain as any other company expense, as set out in the employee
handbook.

### 6.3 Travel to the office

An employee who lives far from their assigned office and travels there for a team
event is reimbursed for travel and one night of accommodation, again under the
handbook expense rules. An employee who chooses to travel to the office for their
normal anchor days, when they could work remotely, does so at their own cost unless
their manager agrees otherwise.

---

## 7. Equipment and security

Remote employees must follow the information security policy in full. In particular,
any laptop used from home must be enrolled in mobile device management, and any
connection to internal Aurora Labs systems must go through the company virtual
private network. Employees must not use a shared or public computer to access
company systems, and must not store company data on personal devices outside the
approved applications.

---

## 9. Accessibility and inclusion

Aurora Labs will make reasonable adjustments to a home workspace where an employee
has a medical or accessibility need that the standard equipment list in section 3.1
does not meet. Adjustments are arranged through People Operations and are funded by
the company outside the annual equipment limits, because they are a workplace
requirement rather than a preference. Requests are confidential and are not shared
with the employee's team.

Where a remote employee needs assistive software, screen readers, or specialised
input devices, the IT department provides these on request and they do not count
against the equipment limits either.

---

## 10. Review

This policy is reviewed every twelve months by People Operations, or sooner if the
company's working arrangements change materially. Employees are notified of changes
at least thirty days before they take effect.
