# Aurora Labs Benefits Guide

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All regular full-time and part-time employees of Aurora Labs
**Owner:** People Operations

This guide describes the benefits Aurora Labs provides to its employees. Read it
alongside the employee handbook, which covers working practices, expenses and the
administrative steps you must follow to request certain leaves. Where this guide
describes how much of a benefit you receive, the handbook often describes how to
ask for it.

---

## 1. Eligibility

Regular full-time employees become eligible for the benefits in this guide on their
first day of employment. Regular part-time employees, defined as those scheduled to
work thirty hours or more per week, receive the same benefits on a pro rata basis
unless a section states otherwise.

Fixed-term contractors and agency staff are not eligible for the benefits in this
guide. Contractors are engaged through their own companies and are responsible for
their own insurance, retirement savings and leave.

Interns receive the wellness stipend described in section 5 and are not eligible for
any other benefit in this guide.

---

## 2. Health plans

Aurora Labs funds three health plan tiers. The company pays a fixed amount toward
the premium of whichever tier an employee chooses, and the employee pays the
difference. Employees select a tier during onboarding and may change tier once per
year during the open enrolment window in November.

### 2.1 Tiers and employee contributions

| Tier | Plan type | Individual deductible | Family deductible | Employee pays per month |
|------|-----------|-----------------------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| Core | High deductible, health savings account included | 3,200 USD | 6,400 USD | 45 USD |
| Plus | Moderate deductible, copays for primary care | 1,500 USD | 3,000 USD | 110 USD |
| Premier | Low deductible, lowest copays, widest network | 500 USD | 1,000 USD | 215 USD |

The employee contribution is deducted monthly from gross pay. The company
contributes the balance of the premium, which is 640 USD per month for single
coverage and 1,480 USD per month for family coverage, regardless of tier. An
employee who enrolls in family coverage pays the family column amount shown above.

### 2.2 Dependents

A spouse or registered partner and children up to the age of twenty-six may be
covered under family coverage. A dependent who is covered by another employer's plan
may still be enrolled, but the employee must confirm that Aurora Labs is the payer
of last resort by completing the coordination of benefits form.

### 2.3 Dental and vision

Dental and vision coverage are separate from the three medical tiers. Dental costs
20 USD per month for single coverage and 48 USD per month for family coverage, and
covers two cleanings per year plus restorative work subject to a 1,500 USD annual
maximum. Vision costs 9 USD per month for single coverage and 22 USD per month for
family coverage, and covers one eye exam per year plus a 180 USD frame or contact
lens allowance.

## 3. Retirement

Aurora Labs operates a retirement savings plan with an employer match. The plan
opens to all employees automatically after ninety days of employment, and the first
contribution is taken from the pay run that follows the eligibility date.

### 3.1 Match formula

The company matches employee contributions in two steps:

1. One hundred percent of the first three percent of salary that the employee
   contributes.
2. Fifty percent of the next two percent of salary that the employee contributes.

An employee who contributes five percent of salary therefore receives the full
match, worth four percent of salary. An employee who contributes three percent
receives three percent. Contributions above five percent of salary are not matched.
The match is calculated per pay run and deposited on the fifteenth of the following
month.

### 3.2 Vesting

The employer match vests immediately. There is no vesting schedule and no clawback
period. Employee contributions are always the property of the employee.

### 3.3 Investment choices

The plan offers a default balanced fund, three index funds covering domestic equity,
international equity and bonds, and a money market fund. Employees who take no
action are enrolled in the default balanced fund. Employees may change their
allocation at any time through the plan portal.

## 4. Parental leave

Aurora Labs provides paid parental leave to employees who become a parent through
birth, adoption or foster placement. Parental leave is separate from, and does not
reduce, the sick leave or general paid time off described in the employee handbook.

### 4.1 Length of leave

| Circumstance | Paid leave | Pay rate |
|--------------|------------|----------|
| Employee gives birth | 16 weeks | 100 percent of base salary |
| Employee's partner gives birth, or employee becomes a parent through adoption or foster placement | 8 weeks | 100 percent of base salary |

The full entitlement is available to employees who have completed twelve months of
continuous employment by the expected start date of the leave. Employees with less
than twelve months of service receive one week of paid parental leave for each six
months of service, to a maximum of eight weeks.

### 4.2 How the weeks may be taken

The full entitlement must be taken within twelve months of the child's birth,
adoption or placement date. Leave may be taken in one continuous block or in two
separate blocks, provided that the second block begins no later than nine months
after the birth, adoption or placement date. Each block must be a minimum of two
weeks long, except that a block following a medically advised reduction in working
hours may be shorter.

### 4.3 Requests and approvals

The length of parental leave is set by this guide. The steps to request the leave,
the notice period and the documents you must supply are described in the employee
handbook. An employee planning parental leave should read both sections.

### 4.4 Return to work

An employee returning from parental leave returns to the same role and salary they
held before the leave began. An employee who wishes to change their working
arrangements after parental leave, including a permanent change to remote working,
must apply through the flexible working process described in the remote work policy.

## 5. Wellness stipend

Every regular full-time employee receives a wellness stipend of 500 USD per calendar
year. Part-time employees receive a pro rata amount based on their scheduled hours.
Interns receive 150 USD.

The stipend reimburses expenses that support physical or mental wellbeing. Eligible
expenses include gym or fitness studio memberships, meditation and sleep application
subscriptions, fitness class packs, and smoking cessation programmes.

The stipend does not cover home office equipment. Desks, chairs, monitors and
similar equipment are reimbursed under the remote work policy, which sets its own
list and its own limits. The stipend also does not cover medical costs, which are
handled through the health plans in section 2, and does not cover general
entertainment or travel.

To claim, submit the receipt through the expense system under the wellness category
before the end of the calendar year in which the expense was incurred. The stipend
does not roll over. An employee who leaves Aurora Labs part way through a year
forfeits the unused portion of that year's stipend.

---

## 6. Learning and development

Every regular full-time employee receives a learning and development budget of
1,200 USD per fiscal year, which runs from 1 April to 31 March. The budget
reimburses course fees, conference tickets, certification exams and professional
books.

The budget may not be used for degree programmes, and a single item costing more
than 600 USD requires the employee's manager to confirm in writing that the item
supports the employee's current role or agreed development goal before the expense
is incurred.

Unused budget does not roll over between fiscal years. Employees who leave part way
through a fiscal year forfeit the unused portion.

## 7. Time off and leave overview

This section summarises the leave an employee is entitled to. The administrative
steps for each type of leave, where they exist, are in the employee handbook.

| Leave type | Entitlement | Notes |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| Paid time off | Accrues at 10 hours per month for full-time employees | See handbook for carryover rules |
| Sick leave | 5 paid days per year, available from the first day | Does not reduce paid time off |
| Parental leave | 16 or 8 weeks depending on circumstance | See section 4 |
| Caregiver leave | 5 paid days per year | For caring for an ill immediate family member |
| Bereavement leave | 3 paid days for an immediate family member, 1 paid day for an extended family member | Extendable by agreement |

Caregiver leave is not the same as parental leave and the two cannot be combined or
converted into one another. Caregiver leave is intended for short-term care of an
ill family member, while parental leave is intended for the arrival of a child
through birth, adoption or foster placement.

## 8. Questions

Questions about any benefit in this guide should go to People Operations through the
help desk. Questions about how to claim an expense should follow the process in the
employee handbook, and questions about remote working equipment should follow the
remote work policy.
